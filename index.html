1. About ZYNOVA AI

ZYNOVA AI ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android AI assistant developed and published by Alamgir App Studio (package name com.alamgirappstudio.zynovaai). It lets you chat with an AI, work with documents and images, use voice, and keep personal notes and memories, either with an AI that runs on your own phone or with Google's online Gemini service.

This Privacy Policy describes what information the app handles, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. How the app works

2.1 Start-up and the welcome page

When you open ZYNOVA AI you see a short splash screen. The first time you use the app, a welcome page explains how the app works: it is made mainly for Offline AI, you download one offline model file, Online AI gives a limited number of answers every day, and Offline AI is unlimited. The welcome page also has a button to download the model that suits your phone. You can skip the download and do it later in the Model Center. The app is free to use and has no account, sign-up or subscription.

2.2 The Main screen

The Main screen is a home for all modules: Smart Chat, Voice Studio, Document Studio, AI Tools, Personality Studio, Knowledge Brain, Memory, Model Center, Device AI Lab, Language Hub, Chat History, Privacy Center and Settings.

2.3 Smart Chat and the two modes

  • Offline (default) – your question is answered by an AI model stored on your phone. Nothing leaves your device, and there is no limit on how much you can use it. The app always opens in Offline mode.
  • Online – your question is answered by Google Gemini. This needs internet and your permission. Online AI has a daily limit of 5 answers (tokens) per day. Each Online answer uses one token.

When all 5 Online answers for the day are used, the app automatically switches to Offline mode, the Online button turns pale and does not work for the rest of that day, and the tokens come back the next day. The number of tokens used and the date are counted and stored only on your phone. A badge shows which AI answered. When the answering AI changes during a chat, the app passes a short hand-off note so the new AI understands the conversation.

Offline AI cannot read images or files; those need Online AI.

2.4 Your permission for Online AI

Before the first Online answer the app asks whether you allow Online AI. If you decline, the app uses Offline AI only. You can change this at any time in the Privacy Center or with the Offline privacy mode switch in the Model Center. Offline privacy mode keeps the app fully offline.

4. Information we handle

4.1 Information you create in the app (stored on your phone)

  • Chat messages and AI answers, chat titles, folders, favorites, pins and lock settings.
  • Memories, "About me", custom instructions, custom personality and offline instructions.
  • Notes and the text of files you add to Knowledge Brain.
  • App settings, such as chat mode, answer depth and style, answer language, voice language, Incognito and permission choices.
  • The result of your last performance test.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive a copy.

4.2 Information sent to Google when you use Online AI

If you allow Online AI, the following may be sent to Google's Gemini API to produce an answer:

  • Your message and the recent conversation (the app limits how much history is sent).
  • The app's instructions to the AI, which can include your selected personality, style, answer language, custom instructions, "About me" and your memories.
  • Files you attach in Document Studio: images or PDFs, and extracted text.
  • Matching excerpts from Knowledge Brain when you use Ask My Files.
  • When Current Information mode or Verification is on, the request also uses Google Search.

4.3 Technical information handled by your phone

To protect your phone and choose the right AI tier, the app reads, on your device only: total and free RAM, number of CPU cores, Android version, processor type, free storage, battery level, temperature and thermal state, and whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

4.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account details.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, SMS or photos library.
  • We do not use advertising IDs and we show no ads.
  • We do not include analytics, tracking or profiling tools.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your chats, files or memories.

5. Online AI and Google Gemini

Online AI is powered by the Google Gemini API (Google AI Studio). When you use Online AI, your request travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection directly from your phone to Google. It does not pass through a server of ours.

The app uses a Gemini API key that belongs to Alamgir App Studio. Because of this, Google may associate requests with our API project. Google processes your request under its own terms and privacy policy, which may include logging and, depending on the Gemini API terms in force for the key and region, using or reviewing content to provide and improve its services. Please read:

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy
Please do not send passwords, bank or card numbers, national ID numbers, medical records or other highly sensitive information to Online AI. If you need to work with sensitive text, use Offline mode, which never leaves your phone.

Web research and sources

When Current Information mode is on, Gemini may use Google Search. The answer can then show the sources that were used and Google's required search suggestions. Opening a source or a suggestion opens it in your browser, where that website's own privacy rules apply.

Fallback to Offline AI

If Online AI fails (no internet or a server problem), or when your 5 Online answers for the day are used, the app answers with Offline AI instead, if the offline model is installed.

6. Offline AI and the model download

Offline AI uses a Gemma model that runs directly on your phone through the LiteRT-LM engine (Google AI Edge). The app checks your phone's RAM and offers only one model, the one that suits your phone:

  • Phones with less RAM: Gemma 3 1B (about 560 MB). It answers in English only.
  • Phones with more RAM: Gemma 4 E2B (about 2.4 GB). It can answer in many languages.

In Offline mode:

  • Your messages are processed on your phone's processor (CPU by default; GPU only if you turn it on).
  • Nothing is sent to the internet.
  • The model is loaded into memory only when needed and is released automatically when the phone is low on memory or the app goes to the background.

Downloading the model

The model file (about 560 MB or 2.4 GB, depending on your phone) is not bundled in the app. You download it from inside the app (Model Center). The file is hosted on Hugging Face (huggingface.co) in the Alamgir App Studio repository. When you download it, Hugging Face and its content delivery network can see your IP address and basic technical request details, as with any website download. No chat content or personal data is sent with the download.

The download runs as a foreground service with a notification, can be paused and resumed, checks that you have enough free storage, warns you before using mobile data, and verifies the file with a SHA-256 checksum so that a damaged or altered file is rejected.

You can delete the model at any time from the Model Center. If the app finds a model file that your phone does not need, it deletes that file to save storage.

7. Voice features

Voice Studio and the microphone button in chat let you speak instead of typing, and let the app read answers aloud.

  • Speech-to-text. ZYNOVA AI does not record or store your voice. Speech recognition is performed by the speech service installed on your phone (for example, Google's speech recognition). Depending on your phone and your language, that service may process your audio on the device or send it to its provider's servers. In Offline mode the app asks the service to prefer offline recognition; this works only if an offline language pack is installed on your phone. Your phone's speech service has its own privacy policy.
  • Text-to-speech. Answers are read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech engine. The text is spoken on your device by the engine you have installed.
  • The recognized text is treated like anything else you type: it is sent to Online AI only when an Online answer is used.
  • The microphone permission is requested only when you use Voice Studio. You can refuse it or revoke it in your phone's settings.

8. Documents, photos and OCR

  • Choosing files. You pick files with Android's system file picker. The app can read only the files you select. It does not browse your storage.
  • Camera. "Take a photo" opens your phone's camera app. The app receives only the picture you take, saved temporarily in its private cache. The app does not need, and does not request, the camera permission.
  • Text reading (OCR). Text is read from images and PDF pages on your phone using Google ML Kit text recognition. The recognition model is bundled with the app, so this works offline and the image is not uploaded for OCR.
  • Image size. Large photos are scaled down before use to save memory.
  • PDFs. For Offline AI the app renders PDF pages on your phone and reads them with OCR (up to the first 8 pages). For Online AI the PDF itself (up to about 12 MB) may be sent to Google so that Gemini can read the whole document.
  • Saving. Document text is saved in the app only if you choose "Save to Knowledge Brain". The original files are not copied into the app.

9. Memory and Knowledge Brain

Memory holds short facts such as your name, job or goals. It is stored only on your phone and is used in every chat while Memory is turned on. You decide what is saved. The app saves a memory only when you add it, or when you write a message that starts with "Remember that…" or "Remember:" (and not while Incognito is on or Memory is turned off). You can delete single memories or all of them in the Memory screen.

Knowledge Brain keeps your notes and file text on your phone and searches them locally when you ask a question. Only the short matching parts are given to the AI.

Remember that anything in your memories or "About me" is part of the instructions sent to Online AI when Online AI is used.

13. Storage, security and backups

  • Private app storage. Your chats, memories and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access on a non-rooted phone.
  • Encrypted connections. All network traffic (Gemini and the model download) uses HTTPS.
  • Private chat lock. You can lock individual chats. Opening a locked chat requires your phone's screen lock (PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint). The text of a locked chat is hidden from the history preview and from search. To use this feature your phone must have a screen lock set up.
  • No automatic cloud backup. The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so your chats are not copied to Google Drive by the system.
  • Backup and export files. If you create a backup or export your data, the file is saved where you choose. These files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe, and delete them when you no longer need them.
  • No method is 100% secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security, especially if your phone is lost, unlocked or infected with malware.

14. Your choices and rights

You control your information from inside the app:

  • Choose Offline-only. Decline Online AI or switch on Offline privacy mode and nothing leaves your phone.
  • Incognito mode. Chats are not saved while Incognito is on.
  • Memory controls. Add, delete or forget all memories, or turn Memory off.
  • View. The Privacy Center shows how many chats, memories and files are stored.
  • Export. "Export all my data" creates a readable JSON file of your chats, memories and saved files.
  • Delete. Delete single chats, all chats, or use "Clear ALL data" to erase chats, memories, files, folders and settings.
  • Permissions. You can revoke the microphone or notification permission in your phone's settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your chats or an account for you, the tools above let you exercise these rights directly. For requests about data held by Google, please contact Google using its privacy tools. If you have any other request, contact us using the details in Section 20.

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not carry out automated decision-making that has legal effects on you.

15. Data retention and deletion

  • Your chats, memories, files and settings stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data from your phone, including the offline model. Files you exported or backed up yourself stay where you saved them.
  • "Clear ALL data" in the Privacy Center erases the app's chats, memories, files, folders and settings immediately.
  • Data that was sent to Google for an Online answer is kept according to Google's own retention rules and cannot be deleted from inside our app.

16. Children's privacy

ZYNOVA AI is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, or the minimum age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no accounts and keeps data on the device, we do not hold information about anyone's age. Parents and guardians should supervise how children use AI tools. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, you can delete all data with "Clear ALL data" or uninstall the app, and you can contact us.

17. AI-generated content

AI answers can be wrong, out of date, incomplete or biased, even when they sound confident. The Offline model is smaller than online models and may make more mistakes. Please check important facts, especially for medical, legal, financial, safety or academic matters. ZYNOVA AI is not a substitute for a doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or other professional. The Verification feature can help, but it also does not guarantee accuracy.

18. International data transfers

If you use Online AI, your request is processed by Google on servers that may be located in countries other than yours. Google applies its own safeguards for such transfers. If you prefer that your data does not leave your device, use Offline mode.
